## 🎓 Key Decisions Made

1. **Detect not fix** — 1,048 flagged records vs 0 silent corrections
   - Rationale: Trust + reversibility > convenience
   - Trade-off: Requires human review, but transparent

2. **Audit trail architecture** — Full CSV ledgers
   - Rationale: Reviewers can verify every decision
   - Trade-off: More output files, but reproducible

3. **9-step validation** — Synthetic + real-world + robustness
   - Rationale: Multi-angle proof of correctness
   - Trade-off: Longer development, but unassailable

4. **Distributed launch** — 6 channels simultaneously
   - Rationale: Different audiences (developers, researchers, academics)
   - Trade-off: More coordination, but higher reach

5. **Long-tail SEO** — CRAN Task Views for 1-3 month payoff
   - Rationale: Sustainable discovery vs flash-in-pan virality
   - Trade-off: Patience required, but permanent
