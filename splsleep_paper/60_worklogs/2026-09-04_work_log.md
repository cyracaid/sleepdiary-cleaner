### Why FLAGGED_UNRESOLVED is High (98.2%/96.8%)

This is **expected and intended behavior**, not a bug:

1. **Design intent**: The Part A4 patch was designed to flag suspicious clock-time-shaped duration entries for human review, NOT to auto-correct them. Silent misrepair (96% in v1.4.0) was the bug - the pipeline was guessing wrong.

2. **FLAGGED_UNRESOLVED = caught & routed to human review**. This is the intended safe behavior:
   - CORRECT (1.8%/3.2%): auto-corrected with high confidence
   - FLAGGED_UNRESOLVED (98.2%/96.8%): routed to human review for expert judgment
   - MISREPAIRED (0%): silent misrepair eliminated

3. **Why so high?**: The field-misentry errors (clock time entered in duration field) are inherently ambiguous - the pipeline cannot reliably distinguish "user meant 11:30 as 11h30min vs 11:30 as clock time" without human context. The patch correctly routes these to human review.

4. **"96.5% caught" misconception**: Earlier docs conflated FLAGGED_UNRESOLVED with auto-correction. The pipeline now routes 96-98% to human review (safe), vs silently misrepairing (0%).
